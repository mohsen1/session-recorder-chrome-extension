Features

Session Recorder covers one workflow: record a bug, point at what is wrong, and hand it to whoever fixes it. Everything below serves that path.

Works on its own

These need only the extension. No account with us, no subscription, nothing sent anywhere unless you export it.

  • Browser recordingClicks, typing, navigation, console, network and new tabs on one timeline.
  • AnnotationsClick an element or drag a rectangle, then say what is wrong. Plus screenshot markup.
  • Screenshots and videoAutomatic or on-demand screenshots, near-duplicates skipped, and optional tab video with audio.
  • Voice notes and transcriptionNarrate while you reproduce; transcripts land on the timeline with your own provider key.
  • Local storage and redactionSessions live in your browser. Secrets are masked at capture time, with honest limits.
  • Exports and AI handoffA ZIP with Markdown, JSON and assets at four detail levels, ready for people or coding agents.

With your own accounts

Configure these once with your credentials. Data goes directly to the services you choose, and their pricing and limits apply.

  • Voice notes and transcriptionNarrate while you reproduce; transcripts land on the timeline with your own provider key.
  • AI issue summariesAnnotation-first issue drafts and automatic session names, using OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or a compatible endpoint.
  • One-click GitHub issuesFile into one of several repositories, each with its own token, with progress and cancel.
  • Protected report publishingPublish to your own Cloudflare Pages project behind Cloudflare Access login.

Voice notes appear in both lists: recording audio is local, and transcription uses your provider key.

Record your next bug instead of describing it

Free and open source. Recording and ZIP export need no account with us. AI, transcription and publishing are optional and use your own providers, whose charges apply.
